SOHO — Small Office & Home Office Support

From a two-person startup to a hundred-user office, SOHO support covers the everyday technical needs that keep a small team running.

Call (800) 871-1703 Request a Quote

What's included

We offer premium technical support to small office and home office users in the DFW area and surrounding cities, as well as to individuals. We support businesses running from two to a hundred users across a range of platforms, including their VoIP systems.

Support is available online or onsite, depending on what the issue calls for and how quickly you need it resolved.

  • Repair & service for desktop computers and laptops
  • Wired & wireless network cabling and configuration
  • Audio/visual cabling and home Bluetooth installation
  • File, photo, audio & video recovery
  • Round-the-clock 24/7 support
  • Online or onsite, based on what you need
