Break-Fix / New Installs

Most managed-service companies steer you away from break-fix because it doesn't lock you into a contract. We think preventative maintenance matters — and that you shouldn't need a retainer to get a fast fix.

Call (800) 871-1703 Request a Quote

What's included

Break-fix is built for the moment something stops working: the computer won't come on, a hard drive needs replacing, network equipment drops offline, or the phones go down. These situations need an immediate response, not a ticket queue.

We handle break-fix and new installs side by side, so the same team that gets you running again today can also handle planned equipment installs when you're ready to upgrade.

  • Rapid response to hardware failures
  • Hard drive replacement
  • Network equipment troubleshooting & repair
  • Phone system outages
  • New equipment installs
  • No long-term service contract required
