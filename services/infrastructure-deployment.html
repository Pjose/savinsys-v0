IT Infrastructure Deployment & Relocation

Whether it's a single server refresh or a multi-site relocation, SIS plans, executes, and documents the move so your infrastructure comes back online on schedule.

Call (800) 871-1703 Request a Quote

What's included

SIS supports managed hosting companies and their clients with corporate IT redeployment, alongside small and medium-sized businesses — dental practices, insurance offices, convenience stores, retail chains, and pharmacies with anywhere from two to fifty employees.

That range means we're equally comfortable staging new enterprise hardware for a data-center client and disconnecting a two-person office ahead of a weekend move. Every job runs on the same change-request discipline larger enterprises expect, executed by technicians who show up on time and document what they touched.

  • Deployment of new enterprise hardware and software
  • Disconnect & reconnect for desktops and laptops
  • Disconnect & reconnect for servers
  • Full office relocations, coordinated end to end
  • VoIP & PBX system relocation, setup, configuration & testing
  • Project-managed timelines for multi-site rollouts
