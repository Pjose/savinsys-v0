Who we are

The team onsite when it matters most

SIS is a leading infrastructure IT support company running structured cabling, deploying smart-hands technicians, and setting up VoIP and networks for medium-sized and small home office setups across the DFW Metroplex.

We remediate stalled network gear, broken cable runs, unreachable servers, and other IT equipment issues in an expedited, agile way — while employing the same change-request methodologies used in the enterprise. Large MSPs rely on us because we're the emergency team found onsite when disaster strikes.

Our in-house field technicians and project managers are multi-state and OEM-certified, and they carry that same discipline across every service line: infrastructure deployment, structured cabling, break-fix, data recovery, VoIP, and small office/home office support.
